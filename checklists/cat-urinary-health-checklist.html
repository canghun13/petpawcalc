Frequently Asked Questions

How many litter boxes should I have?

One per cat plus one extra, and spread around the home rather than lined up in a row — cats can read a cluster of boxes in one room as a single resource. Even in a one-cat household two boxes gives a cat choice, which matters more than it sounds when the goal is reducing stress. Adding a litter box is one of the changes owners most commonly adopt when following multimodal environmental modification advice, partly because it is easy and partly because it works.

Does wet food really help with cat urinary problems?

Increasing water intake is one of the better-supported interventions, and food is the most reliable way to do it because you are not depending on the cat choosing to drink. Veterinary guidance on managing lower urinary tract disease recommends gradually transitioning to a moist diet above roughly 60% moisture alongside other strategies for increasing water intake. More dilute urine is less irritating to the bladder wall and less prone to forming crystals. Change the diet gradually over 7 to 10 days rather than overnight.

What is MEMO for cats?

Multimodal environmental modification — a structured approach to reducing stress in cats with feline idiopathic cystitis, developed at Ohio State. A clinical evaluation by Buffington and colleagues, published in the Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery in 2006, found that cats whose owners implemented it showed reductions not only in urinary signs but in fearfulness and other stress-related problems, which is part of why FIC is now understood as a broader stress-response condition rather than a purely bladder one.

Can stress really cause urinary problems in cats?

For feline idiopathic cystitis, stress is understood to play a key role — the AVMA states directly that stress or anxiety can increase the risk and severity of FIC, and that the main treatment approach involves changing the cat's environment to increase their sense of control and reduce their sense of threat. Common triggers include a new pet or person, building work, a move, changes in routine, and conflict in multi-cat households. That does not make the pain any less real or mean the cat does not need veterinary care.

Should I put my cat on a urinary prescription diet?

Only if your veterinarian prescribes one. Urinary diets are formulated to target particular stone or crystal types, and the wrong one can be unhelpful or counterproductive — which is exactly why they are prescription products rather than something to pick off a shelf. They also need to be matched to the cat's other conditions, since a diet aimed at urinary health may not suit a cat with kidney disease. Bring the question to your vet rather than switching independently.

What are the emergency signs I should never wait on?

Straining in the litter box while producing little or no urine, crying out while trying to urinate, or urinary signs alongside vomiting, lethargy or hiding. Any of those means calling an emergency clinic immediately rather than waiting for morning, particularly in a male cat. A complete urethral obstruction becomes life-threatening within roughly 24 to 48 hours, and it is very commonly mistaken for constipation because the two look almost identical.
