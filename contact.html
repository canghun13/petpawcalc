Contact Us

Have a question about one of our tools? Spotted a calculation error? Want to suggest a new tool? We'd love to hear from you.

We're a small independent project, but we read every message and genuinely appreciate feedback from pet owners. Whether it's a quick question or a detailed suggestion, don't hesitate to reach out.

Get in Touch

Send us an email at: canghun13@naver.com

We typically respond within 2–3 business days.

What We Can Help With

  • Questions about how our calculators work
  • Reporting errors or outdated information
  • Suggesting new tools or features
  • Blog topic requests
  • General feedback

What We Can't Help With

We're not veterinarians and can't provide medical advice for your pet. If your pet is unwell, please contact your local veterinarian directly. For emergencies, contact an emergency animal hospital immediately.

Response Times

We aim to respond to all messages within 2–3 business days. During busy periods it may take a little longer, but we'll always get back to you.
