Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

PetPawCalc ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

PetPawCalc does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our tools. All calculations are performed in your browser — we do not store your inputs on our servers.

We may collect the following non-personal data automatically:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages (via Google Analytics)
  • Browser type and device type
  • Referring website
  • General geographic region (country/city level)

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense). These cookies help us understand how visitors use our site and allow Google to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of Google Analytics via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Contact Forms

If you contact us via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's Privacy

PetPawCalc is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
