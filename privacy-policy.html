Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

PetPawCalc ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

PetPawCalc does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our tools. All calculations are performed in your browser — we do not store your inputs on our servers.

We may collect the following non-personal data automatically:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages (via Google Analytics)
  • Browser type and device type
  • Referring website
  • General geographic region (country/city level)

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense). These cookies help us understand how visitors use our site and allow Google to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of Google Analytics via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your Privacy Choices (US State Privacy Laws)

Residents of some US states, including California, have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, which can include the use of advertising cookies for personalized ads. You can exercise this choice at any time using the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link shown on our pages, which is provided through Google's privacy messaging. You can also opt out of personalized advertising through the Google Ad Settings and aboutads.info links above.

Information Saved in Your Browser

A few features save small amounts of information in your own browser's local storage so they work the next time you visit — for example, checklist progress, a log of breathing-rate readings you choose to save, or whether you last viewed dog or cat content on the home page. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

If You Email Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you and to improve the site, and we don't sell it or share it with third parties.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested, for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's Privacy

PetPawCalc is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
