Common advice, checked against the research

Some of what owners are told doesn't hold up. These are the corrections our guides are built on.

Puppies need five minutes of exercise per month of age.

No study is behind that number. In a study of 501 large-breed puppies, off-lead play on soft ground went with lower hip dysplasia risk.

Read the puppy exercise guide

A negative heartworm test means your dog is clear.

The test can't detect an infection until it is roughly seven months old, so it reports on the past.

Read about heartworm testing

Daily pills are the cheap way to treat a hyperthyroid cat.

Pills plus rechecks usually overtake a one-time radioiodine cure within one to three years.

See the cost comparison

A heart murmur in a cat means heart disease.

In 780 healthy-looking cats, about 70% of murmurs were harmless, and some cats with heart disease had none.

Read about cat heart murmurs
