Why the 7x Rule Is Wrong

Dogs age much faster in their early years and slower later on. A 1-year-old dog is roughly equivalent to a 15-year-old human, not a 7-year-old. Size also matters — large breeds age faster than small breeds.

This calculator uses size-adjusted aging curves based on published veterinary research to give you a more accurate result.
