🐱 Cat Age Calculator

Cats age very differently from humans — especially in the first two years. Enter your cat's age below to get an accurate human year equivalent.

How Cat Aging Works

A cat's first year equals roughly 15 human years. Their second year adds about 9 more. After that, each cat year is approximately 4 human years.

This calculator follows the guidelines developed by the American Association of Feline Practitioners (AAFP).
