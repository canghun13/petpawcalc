Comparing Like With Like

  • Heartworm. Collars, Frontline Plus and NexGard don't prevent heartworm. In most of the US a dog also needs a heartworm preventive — a year of a popular monthly heartworm chew for a 51–100 lb dog was about $146 at the same retailer — so compare that total with a combination product such as Simparica Trio. Treating heartworm usually costs $700–$2,000 or more (see Heartworm Treatment Cost).
  • Year-round use. The Companion Animal Parasite Council recommends year-round, lifelong flea and tick prevention: fleas may already have laid eggs by the time you see them, ticks can transmit disease before they're found, and seasons are shifting and vary by region.
  • Cats. The ASPCA warns never to use a dog product on a cat; some dog products contain permethrin, which is highly toxic to cats.
  • Yearly injection for dogs. Bravecto Quantum, approved by the FDA in July 2025, is a vet-given injection that protects dogs 6 months and older for 12 months against fleas and most ticks (8 months for lone star ticks). Its price is set by each clinic.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does flea and tick prevention cost per year?

At Chewy prices checked in November 2026, a year of protection for a 23–44 lb dog cost about $87 with a Seresto collar, $138 with Frontline Plus, $336 with NexGard and $456 with Simparica Trio, which also prevents heartworm. For a cat, it was about $90 with a Seresto collar, $150 with Frontline Plus and $309 with Revolution Plus (5.6–11 lb).

Is a flea and tick collar cheaper than monthly prevention?

Per year, usually yes — one Seresto collar lasts about 8 months, so a year is about one and a half collars. But a collar doesn't prevent heartworm, so in most of the US you'd also need a heartworm preventive. A year of a popular monthly heartworm chew for a 51–100 lb dog was about $146 at the same retailer.

Does dog size affect flea and tick prevention cost?

It depends on the product. Products are sold by weight band, but some are priced almost the same across bands — NexGard was about $164–$170 for six chews in every band at Chewy — while others vary more: Simparica Trio was about $189 for six chews for 11–22 lb dogs and $235 for 44–88 lb dogs.
