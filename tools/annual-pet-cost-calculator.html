💰 Annual Pet Cost Calculator

Build a yearly budget line by line. The calculator fills in sourced US averages where they exist and asks you for the costs that depend on your own choices.

🐾 PetPawCalc

⚕️ Averages are US national figures from the sources named; your local prices may be higher or lower. Emergencies are not included.

Where Each Number Comes From

  • Routine vet care uses national averages from a 2025–2026 study of veterinary practices run for CareCredit by Synchrony: an exam ($56–$129 for dogs, $57–$130 for cats; the calculator uses the midpoint), the vaccines typically due in a year, and for seniors a chemistry panel, a blood count for dogs and a second exam. PetMD's veterinarian-written price guide supplies the heartworm test ($20–$75), fecal exam ($25–$50) and urinalysis ($20–$60), at their midpoints. PetMD recommends yearly checkups for adults and six-monthly checkups for seniors.
  • Parasite prevention is a year of Simparica Trio for dogs (fleas, ticks, heartworm and some intestinal worms) or Revolution Plus for cats, at Chewy prices checked in November 2026. See Flea and Tick Prevention Cost for cheaper and different options.
  • Insurance uses the North American Pet Health Insurance Association's 2025 average premiums: $836 (dogs) and $435 (cats) for accident and illness, $190 and $112 for accident only.
  • Supplies are the ASPCA's 2021 figures for treats, toys and a license, plus litter for cats ($150). The default boarding rate of $25 a night is also the ASPCA's 2021 figure and is low for many areas — replace it with your local price.
  • Dental cleaning, if selected, is Synchrony's average: $379 for a dog, $395 for a cat, before X-rays or extractions.
  • Food and grooming vary too much by size, coat and product for an average to mean much, so enter what you spend. The ASPCA's 2021 table used $300 a year for dog food and $225 for cat food.

What the Survey Figures Say

Rover's 2026 cost of pet ownership report puts a medium-sized dog at about $145–$505 a month and a medium-sized cat at about $70–$305, with median lifetime costs of $39,350 and $32,527. It found small-breed adult dogs cost about 63% less a year than large and giant breeds, and that senior years cost the most: about $2,475–$5,200 a year for a dog and $2,255–$4,740 for a cat. In its survey, 83% of owners had noticed pet costs rising over the past year.

What This Doesn't Include

Emergencies and illness. Approximate costs at emergency hospitals, compiled by Preventive Vet, include $100–$200 for the emergency exam, $600–$1,700 for one to two days in hospital and $1,500–$5,000 for emergency surgery. Insurance, savings or both are the usual ways to plan for these — see How to Reduce Vet Costs for Dogs and for Cats.

The first year also costs more than later ones because of one-time items. The ASPCA's 2021 table puts a dog's first year at $3,221 and a cat's at $1,904, including spay or neuter, the initial vaccine series and equipment — and spay and neuter prices have risen since (Synchrony's averages are $455–$487 for a small or medium dog and $262–$353 for a cat).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to own a dog per year?

Rover's 2026 cost of pet ownership report puts a medium-sized dog at about $145–$505 a month, and a senior dog at about $2,475–$5,200 a year. The ASPCA's cost table, last updated in 2021, has a typical year at $1,391. Size, age, insurance and services such as boarding and grooming account for most of the difference.

How much does it cost to own a cat per year?

Rover's 2026 report puts a medium-sized cat at about $70–$305 a month and a senior cat at about $2,255–$4,740 a year. The ASPCA's 2021 table has a typical year at $1,149, with insurance, food, routine care and litter as the largest lines.

Where do the calculator's numbers come from?

Vet prices are national averages from a 2025–2026 study run for CareCredit by Synchrony, with PetMD's ranges for tests it doesn't list; parasite prevention uses Chewy prices checked in November 2026; insurance uses NAPHIA's 2025 average premiums; supplies and the default boarding rate use the ASPCA's 2021 cost table. Food and grooming depend so much on the pet and product that you enter your own figures.
