What the Veterinary Bodies Say

The American Veterinary Medical Association's policy is that it discourages feeding any raw or undercooked animal-source protein to cats and dogs, and supports diets processed in ways that reduce or eliminate the risk of illness from pathogens. It also notes that apparently healthy dogs and cats can carry these organisms without showing illness.

The FDA's Center for Veterinary Medicine tested 196 raw pet food samples in 2010–2012: 15 were positive for Salmonella and 32 for Listeria monocytogenes. Among other types of pet food, one dry cat food sample tested positive for Salmonella and none for Listeria.

If You Do Feed Raw: FDA Handling Advice

  • Wash hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds after handling raw food or anything it touched.
  • Clean and disinfect bowls, utensils, cutting boards and counters (the FDA suggests 1 tablespoon of bleach per quart of water).
  • Keep raw food frozen until you use it, and thaw it in the fridge or microwave, not on the counter.
  • Keep it away from other food, and refrigerate or throw out leftovers promptly.
  • Don't let your pet lick your face, especially after it eats.

How the Amount Is Worked Out

Rather than a fixed percentage of body weight, the calculator estimates calories with the 2021 AAHA Nutrition and Weight Management Guidelines — RER = 70 × (weight in kg)0.75, times AAHA's life-stage factor (for example 1.4–1.6 for a neutered adult dog, 1.2–1.4 for a neutered adult cat, 1.0 × RER at ideal weight for a dog losing weight and 0.8 for a cat) — and divides by your food's calories per pound. Raw foods vary a lot in calories, so the label figure matters.

About 80/10/10: the split option uses a common raw-feeding convention of 80% muscle meat, 10% bone and 10% organ. It's not a veterinary standard, and no ratio on its own makes a homemade diet complete and balanced — ask your vet about a properly formulated recipe.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much raw food should my pet eat a day?

It depends on calories, not a fixed percentage of body weight. This calculator estimates daily calories with the 2021 AAHA guidelines (70 × kg^0.75, times a life-stage factor) and divides by the calories per pound of your raw food, from the label or recipe.

What is the 80/10/10 raw feeding ratio?

A raw-feeding convention — about 80% muscle meat, 10% edible bone and 10% organ — that the calculator can split your daily amount into. It isn't a veterinary standard, and following it doesn't by itself make a diet complete and balanced.

Is raw feeding safe for dogs and cats?

The AVMA discourages feeding any raw or undercooked animal-source protein to dogs and cats because of the risk to pets and people. In an FDA study of 196 raw pet food samples, 15 tested positive for Salmonella and 32 for Listeria monocytogenes, while other pet food types had almost none.

How should I handle raw pet food?

The FDA advises washing hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, disinfecting bowls, utensils and surfaces, keeping raw food frozen until use and thawing it in the fridge or microwave, refrigerating or discarding leftovers promptly, and not letting your pet lick your face after eating.
